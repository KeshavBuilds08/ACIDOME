    The problem

    Acidic rainwater can affect crops, aquatic ecosystems, water resources and infrastructure. Manual testing and delayed communication mean people often get warnings too late.

    Existing methods

    • Manual pH testing: strips or meters.
    • Laboratory analysis: detailed, but needs sample collection and processing.
    • Digital monitoring: sensors for selected parameters.

    Research gap

    • Delayed manual testing
    • Limited instant communication
    • Lack of accessible user alerts
    • Need for location-based monitoring

    How ACIDOME works

    Sensor readings, digital analysis, location-based alerts, user dashboard.

    InputRainwater collection and sensor readings.
    ProcessingESP32 receives and processes sensor data.
    AnalysisEvaluates readings and flags unusual patterns.
    CommunicationSends data to the database and notification service.
    User interfaceDashboard shows readings, history and alerts.

    Farmers

    • Irrigation water monitoring
    • pH and conductivity readings
    • Instant mobile alerts
    • Simple usage guidance

    Industries

    • Rainwater monitoring
    • Water-quality trends
    • Abnormal reading alerts
    • Digital records

    Testing, challenges and limits

    Testing

    • pH sensor reading checks
    • Conductivity checks
    • ESP32 data transmission
    • Dashboard and alert verification

    Challenges

    • Sensor calibration
    • Changing environmental conditions
    • Connectivity interruptions
    • Maintaining accurate readings

    Limitations

    • Measures only selected parameters
    • Cannot identify every pollutant
    • Needs regular sensor maintenance
    • Lab testing may be needed to confirm

    Future scope

    Predictive analysis

    • Identify contamination patterns and potential risks

    Advanced monitoring

    • Integrate additional water-quality sensors

    Wider connectivity

    • Expand to rural areas with multilingual alerts
